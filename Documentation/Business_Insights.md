## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze workplace health and safety incidents to identify major causes, injury patterns, and areas requiring safety improvements.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Event Types

Certain workplace event types contribute significantly to total incidents, indicating recurring operational risks.

---

### 2. Human Factors

Human-related factors remain one of the primary contributors to workplace accidents, highlighting the need for employee training and safety awareness programs.

---

### 3. Nature of Injuries

Some injury types occur far more frequently than others, allowing organizations to prioritize preventive measures.

---

### 4. Injury Severity

The majority of reported incidents are non-fatal, while fatal incidents represent a smaller proportion of total cases.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Conduct regular employee safety training.
- Improve workplace hazard identification.
- Increase compliance with OSHA safety standards.
- Perform periodic safety audits.
- Implement preventive maintenance programs.
- Monitor recurring incident categories through dashboards.

---

## Business Value

The dashboard enables management to:

- Identify high-risk workplace activities
- Monitor injury trends
- Improve workplace safety
- Support data-driven decision making
- Reduce workplace incidents

---

## Dashboard KPIs

- Total Incidents
- Degree of Injury Distribution
- Top Event Types
- Top Human Factors
- Top Nature of Injuries

---

## Conclusion

Using SQL and Power BI, the project transforms raw workplace safety data into actionable business insights that can help organizations improve employee safety and operational efficiency.
